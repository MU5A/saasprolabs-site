# SaasPro Labs

## AI-Powered Infrastructure for Insurance & Healthcare

---

### Who We Are

**SaasPro Labs** builds AI agents and automation infrastructure for insurance companies, HMOs, and healthcare providers across Africa. We help organizations process claims faster, detect fraud smarter, and deliver better customer experiences — all powered by generative AI.

---

### What We Do

| Product | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Pre-Auth AI** | Automates pre-authorization decisions for healthcare providers. Instant eligibility checks, policy validation, and utilization review — with human escalation for edge cases. |
| **Claims AI** | End-to-end claims processing automation. From submission to adjudication, reduce manual work by 80%+. |
| **Extract AI** | Turn medical documents, invoices, and claims forms into structured data instantly using AI-powered document extraction. |
| **Support AI** | Intelligent chatbots and routing for customer support. Handle inquiries, check claim status, and resolve issues 24/7. |
| **Fraud Detection** | ML-powered fraud detection that identifies suspicious patterns, duplicate claims, and anomalies in real-time. |
| **Pay AI** | Automate payout operations, reconciliation, and provider payments with AI-assisted financial workflows. |
